# Weekly brief

## Template (one page)

**North Adriatic offtake radar, week of <date>**

1. **Headline** (two sentences): the one thing that changed or matters most this week.
2. **New signals** (max 5): each with date, what happened, why it matters for demand, and a source link. Mark each *confirmed* or *unverified*.
3. **Buyers to watch** (max 3): installation or company from the data, with the hydrogen or CO2 reason and what would make it move.
4. **Number of the week**: one figure from the model with its formula and assumption (for example the break-even price at current gas and ETS prices).
5. **Open questions for the team**: up to three.

If there were no signals this week, say so. Do not fill space.

## Prompt for the weekly scheduled task

Search for news from the last 7 days that affects hydrogen or e-SAF demand in Slovenia, Croatia and Friuli Venezia Giulia. Look for: European Hydrogen Bank and Innovation Fund results; cancelled, delayed or newly announced hydrogen and e-fuel projects; permits and grid connections; refinery, steel, cement, fertiliser and glass decarbonisation announcements by installations in the project data; changes in EU ETS prices, gas prices or ReFuelEU and RED III rules; airport and fuel-supplier announcements about synthetic fuels. Keep only items with a source link, and mark each as confirmed or unverified. Then write the brief using the template, using the project data for any numbers about installations or airports. Do not invent signals. Output the brief only.

Have a person read it before it is sent outside the team.
