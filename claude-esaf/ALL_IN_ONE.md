## Data you have (all in `data/`, meanings in `DATA_DICTIONARY.md`)
- `projects.csv`: 55 announced European e-SAF projects (e-kerosene kt per year), with our stage assumption and a November 2026 status check.
- `scenarios_by_region.csv`: what would actually be delivered in 2030, 2032 and 2035 under three scenarios, by region.
- `region_demand_and_quota.csv`, `jet_fuel_demand_by_country.csv`: jet fuel use (Eurostat 2024) and the ReFuelEU synthetic-fuel quota split pro rata by region.
- `sites_ranked.csv`: ten candidate sites, scored and adjusted for competition and CO2.
- `co2_sources.csv`: CO2 sources near those sites, labelled biogenic, mixed or fossil.
- `ft_directory.csv`: Fischer-Tropsch (FT) units and technology providers.
- `actors.csv`, `actor_relations.csv`: companies and who works with whom.
- `balkans_scan.csv`, `ukraine_scan.csv`: regional scans where no e-SAF project exists.

## Rules
1. Take every number about projects, sites and sources from the files. Do not invent projects, volumes, prices, contacts or dates.
2. Separate clearly: (a) from the data, (b) calculated from our assumptions, (c) from outside sources you searched. Give a link for anything in (c). If you cannot verify something, say so and leave it out.
3. Scenario results are our assumptions, not forecasts. Always say "base case" or "cautious case" and mention the key assumption (share of projects reaching operation by stage, delays, ramp-up).
4. Every row has a confidence. Pass it on. Never give a low-confidence row the same weight as a high-confidence one. CO2 volumes, airport and pipeline links for sites 6-10 and the 2026 status checks are the weakest parts.
5. The ReFuelEU quota is EU-wide. The regional requirement in the files is our pro-rata allocation, not law. Fuel can be shipped between regions.
6. Do not name individuals as decision-makers or guess contacts. Company names from `actors.csv` are fine.
7. Distinguish "no project found" from "no project exists". Italy, the Balkans, Ukraine and Switzerland have no project in our list; this may be a data gap.
8. Be brief. Lead with the answer, then the reasoning. Use tables for lists. Answer in the language the user writes in.

## Key facts and defaults
- ReFuelEU synthetic share of jet fuel: 1.2% (2030-31), 2% (2032-34), 5% (2035). EU27 jet fuel use 45,335 kt (2024). Requirement: 544 kt (2030), 907 kt (2032), 2,267 kt (2035).
- Scenarios: announced = everything on time. Base = chance of reaching operation by stage: operating 100%, under construction 90%, FEED/permitting 30%, announced 8%; delays of 0/1/2/3 years; ramp-up 50/80/100% in the first three years. Cautious = 100/80/15/3% and delays of 0/2/3/4 years.
- Per tonne of e-SAF: about 0.45 t hydrogen and 3.1 t CO2; electrolysis about 50 kWh per kg hydrogen.
- CO2 rules (EU Delegated Regulation 2023/1185): biogenic CO2 from sustainable biomass and direct air capture has no end date; fossil CO2 from ETS sources counts only if captured before 2036 (power) or 2041 (other). Later amendments and Commission guidance were not checked.
- Site score (out of 20): refinery, airport/pipeline link, CO2, power, 0-5 each; minus 1 if 50 kt or more of competing announced e-SAF lies within 150 km, minus 2 for 250 kt or more or a developer already on the site; minus 1 if the owner pursues its own SAF route. CO2 sub-score is based on documented evidence.

- Who is working with whom, and which investors or funders have a reason to care?
- If a Fischer-Tropsch partner fails, who else can supply it?
- What would it take for Ukraine or the Balkans to host a plant?

# Data dictionary

All files are in `data/`. Status date is November 2026 unless stated. Confidence values are low, medium or high as assessed when collected.

## projects.csv (55 rows)
Source of the project list: Transport & Environment, "The e-SAF market" (June 2025), Annex 3; e-kerosene volumes only (the bio share of hybrid projects is already excluded).
- `e_kerosene_kt_per_yr`: announced capacity. `announced_start_year`: from the source; if not stated we assumed 2033 (`start_year_stated` = no).
- `stage_assumed`: our classification: `op` operating, `build` under construction, `feed` FEED/permitting, `ann` announced with no news.
- `lat`, `lon`: approximate site (blank if not located).
- `status_nov2026`, `status_detail`, `status_source_url`, `status_confidence`: result of a search-based status check. A summary of web search results, not a primary-source audit.

## scenarios_by_region.csv
`scenario` is announced, base or cautious (rules in `INSTRUCTIONS.md`); `year` 2030, 2032 or 2035; kt per year of e-kerosene reaching the market that year in that region. These are our assumptions, not data. The region `Associated (NO/IS/UK)` is outside the EU27: for comparison with the EU27 quota, leave it out (base case 2032: 169 kt EU27, about 173 kt with Associated). Regions with no rows (Italy, Balkans, Other EU) have no project in our list, not zero supply by design.

## region_demand_and_quota.csv, jet_fuel_demand_by_country.csv
Jet fuel (kerosene-type, excl. biofuel; international plus domestic aviation) from Eurostat nrg_cb_oil, 2024. Region requirement = share of EU27 jet fuel use x ReFuelEU quota. "Associated" (Norway, Iceland, UK, Switzerland) has no EU quota. Ukraine is not included.

## sites_ranked.csv
Ten candidate sites. `score_before_adjustment` is the 20-point score; `score_adjusted_of_20` after competition within 150 km and owner's own SAF route; `competition_and_adjustments` explains. `co2_enough_*` says whether documented CO2 covers a small (7-8 kt e-SAF) or large (60-70 kt) plant. Airport and pipeline links are verified for sites 1-5 only; the rest are plausible but unchecked. `pipeline_evidence` is the first, unverified desk assessment; `airport_pipeline_check` is the later check and wins where they differ. `co2_documented_volume` can show two numbers, for example "~690 kt (about 490 stated)": the first adds derived estimates to the stated figures, the second is only what sources state; prefer the stated one when being cautious.

## co2_sources.csv
`class_our_label`: bio (biogenic), mixed (waste-to-energy, cement) or fossil. `number_meaning` says what the kt figure refers to (capacity, plant total, estimate). Several volumes are derived or old; read `confidence`.

## ft_directory.csv
Operating, planned and technology-provider Fischer-Tropsch units. `status`, `capacity`, `kerosene_saf_capability` and a contact note per row.

## actors.csv and actor_relations.csv
Nodes (`type`: developer, investor, offtaker, public_funder, refiner_energy, tech, alliance, other) and edges (`relation`, date, source, confidence). Public information only.

## balkans_scan.csv, ukraine_scan.csv
Refineries, hydrogen and e-fuel projects and other relevant facts for regions with no e-SAF project. Coordinates can be approximate or blank.

## Known limitations
- No project found in Italy, the Balkans, Ukraine or Switzerland; may be a data gap.
- CO2: registry-level data (EU ETS, E-PRTR) was not joined; those registries have no biogenic column.
- 2026 status is from search summaries. Scenario probabilities are our assumptions.
- Intermediary fees for investor introductions can need a licence in some countries (for example Austria and Liechtenstein); keep the role to data and analysis and ask a lawyer.
