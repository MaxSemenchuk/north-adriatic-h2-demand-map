Who in the North Adriatic could physically buy hydrogen, and how much. Prototype v0.2, built only from public data (EU ETS verified emissions 2025, Eurostat airport traffic). Estimates rest on stated assumptions and are not validated.

Price check

Break-even = gas energy cost + avoided CO₂ cost per kg H₂ (gas basis, LHV 33.3 kWh/kg, 6.7 kg CO₂ avoided/kg H₂). Ignores burner/retrofit capex and grid/transport fees, so real break-even is lower.

e-kerosene needs about 0.45 t H₂ and 3.1 t CO₂ per t fuel. The obligation sits with fuel suppliers at EU airports, so these figures are the local share of a market that is EU-wide. Airlines and suppliers can buy e-SAF from anywhere.

How the estimate works

  • H₂ potential = verified CO₂ × share of emissions that is fuel heat (H₂-addressable) × 0.149 t H₂ per t CO₂ (gas-equivalent; ammonia 0.105). Shares per sector are illustrative assumptions (e.g. cement 10%, paper 80%, coal/gas power 15%, steel 45%) and must be calibrated with sector experts.
  • Location comes from the account holder's postcode, not the plant. Several plants (e.g. INA's refinery, HEP plants) are filed under Zagreb; I added name keywords for Rijeka, Plomin, Pula, Koper etc. Check the "Where" column.
  • CO₂ for e-fuel: cement, lime and ammonia emit concentrated process CO₂ (rated High), steel and refining are Medium, combustion and paper are Low. ETS counts fossil CO₂ only, so biogenic CO₂ (e.g. from pulp and paper) is invisible here.
  • Aviation: jet fuel is estimated as departing passengers (half of the total) times the fuel slider, so it is a rough proxy. Synthetic shares follow ReFuelEU Aviation as I recall them; check them against the regulation text.
  • Not covered: maritime fuels and installations outside the ETS (small, below 20 MW).

Sources: European Commission, Union Registry verified emissions 2025 (extract 1 Apr 2026) and list of operators; Eurostat, table avia_paoa (passengers by reporting airport).
