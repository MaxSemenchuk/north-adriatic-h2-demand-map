CO2 is the weakest point. No site has a confirmed biogenic CO2 source with a published volume. Schwedt has a named one (LEIPA paper mill, volume unknown). Cement and refinery CO2 is mostly tied up in carbon-storage projects. Under current EU rules CO2 from fossil industry is accepted only for a limited time, so a lasting plan needs biogenic or air-captured CO2. The rule text was not checked against the official source.
