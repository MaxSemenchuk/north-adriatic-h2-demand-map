Why fuel comes first

  • Demand is set by regulation, not by a buyer's goodwill: fuel suppliers face blending obligations with penalties, as I understand ReFuelEU Aviation (check the regulation text).
  • The synthetic sub-mandate can only be met with hydrogen plus CO₂, so e-fuel is a pull for both.
  • A fuel plant is one counterparty story (a supplier or airline offtake), which is easier to finance than many small industrial hydrogen buyers. The hydrogen page covers the industrial side.

How the estimate works

  • Jet fuel = departing passengers (half of the Eurostat total) × the fuel-per-passenger slider. This is a rough proxy for fuel uplift.
  • Synthetic shares follow ReFuelEU Aviation as I recall them. Check them against the regulation.
  • Only eight regional airports are included: Venice, Treviso, Trieste, Ljubljana, Zagreb, Pula, Rijeka and Zadar.
